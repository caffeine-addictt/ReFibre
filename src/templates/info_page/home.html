About Us

We are ReFiber, a clothing company that sells high quality recycled clothing garment. Founded in 2014, our goal is to reduce the global wastage of textile waste by reusing the discarded textiles and giving them a second chance.

Do You Know?

92 Million tons of textile waste is being produced every single year, this contributes to 7% of the total amount of waste in global landfill space! Many of these textile waste are a result of the fashion industry adopting the trend of fast fashion. Fast fashion describes low-priced but stylish clothing that moves quickly from design to retail stores to meet trends. This results in a high amount of textile waste due to many of these clothes breaking down easily, due to the low quality material, and being discarded quickly.


Why ReFiber?

Here at ReFiber, all of our clothes are made with at least 10% of recycled materials, we source these discarded textiles from clothes thrown away by companies and our partners from SembCorp that helps us sort these discarded textiles. After sourcing our textiles, the clothes will be produced at our factory in Singapore, each piece of garment is meticulously crafted with strict quality control, ensuring a long shelf life and comfortability for you. Unfortunately, only 1% of discarded textiles are recycled, this is due to the fact that many of these fast fashion companies simply using materials that are cannot be recycled and easily thrown away. But this is where we come in. Our mission is to produce clothes that are long lasting and recyclable
